**How to state the anode answer.** Say: *LG's own documents do not describe the anode.* The product
specifications (A/B/C above) never mention electrode chemistry at all. The PSDS (D) is the closest
vendor statement and it lists the anode active material only as "Carbon (proprietary)" at
10–30 wt% with no silicon compound present, which is consistent with graphite — but "graphite" and
"graphite-SiOx" are both absent as words. If a citation needs the words "graphite" or
"graphite-SiOx", no LG document fetched here supplies them.

**Mass-basis warning for the M50 family.** Three LG documents give three different masses:
67.5 ± 1.0 g (M50LT 2021, *without washer*) · 68.2 ± 1.0 g (M50LT 2020, *with tube and washer*) ·
70.0 g max (M50T 2018, *without washer*, tentative). The ~2.5 g spread is worth ~10 Wh/kg.
Use the same document for both numerator and denominator.

**Anode chemistry.** None of the three LG product specifications (A/B/C) states whether the anode is
graphite or graphite-SiOx — they never mention electrode chemistry at all. The only vendor statement
is the PSDS in section **D** above: "Carbon (proprietary)", CAS 7440-44-0, 10–30 wt%, with no
silicon compound anywhere in the composition table.

(`INR18650P28B`, data sheet doc no. 80104, on Molicel's own server). The corresponding 21700 part
in that family is the **INR-21700-P42B** (doc no. 80101). The P28B line in the task list appears to
be a mis-specified model number, so it is not a 21700 and is excluded from this table.

## Method note (why the gaps)

The session's web-search budget was already exhausted when this run started, so discovery relied on
(1) the Internet Archive CDX index to enumerate vendor and re-host PDF paths, and (2) WebFetch
against search engines. Of the engines tried, only Brave Search returned results; it then
rate-limited (HTTP 429) and did not recover. DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Ecosia, Google, Bing (which
redirects to a region-localised host that ignored the query terms), Yahoo and several SearxNG
instances were blocked or returned no results. Marginalia returned results but indexes only small
sites and had nothing for these part numbers.

Consequence: the models above were reached because a vendor or re-host URL could be *enumerated*
(CDX over `molicel.com`, `batteryspace.com`, `dnkpower.com`, `orbtronic.com`, `tme.eu`). Models
whose datasheets exist only behind a search-engine hit, a contact form, or a client-side product
page could not be reached at all. The gaps are a discovery failure, **not** evidence that the vendor
publishes nothing.
