# Panel (a) — Deployment scenarios (photorealistic)

Middle panel: a grid-scale energy storage container site at dusk with lithium battery racks visible through glass doors, warm datacenter-style lighting, utility-scale.

Right panel: a commercial quadcopter drone on a workbench with an engineer holding a small battery cell; a drone flight silhouette above, clear sky.

Style: product-photography, shallow depth of field, consistent lens/lighting/color grading across panels, no text, no labels, no logos, no people's faces, scientific brochure quality.
