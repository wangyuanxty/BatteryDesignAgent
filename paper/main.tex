\title{When Design Decisions Go to the Agent: A Governance Experiment in a Virtual Battery Factory}

\author[inst1]{Naiming Xie}
\author[inst1]{Yuan Wang}

\affiliation[inst1]{organization={College of Economics and Management, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics}, city={Nanjing}, country={China}}

\begin{abstract}
We report a controlled governance experiment in a virtual battery factory: the
first system, to our knowledge, in which a single LLM agent spans the full chain
of cell-design levers---electrolyte, separator, electrode, architecture,
thermal---in one autonomous loop, and in which every verdict is computed rather
than asserted. Eight scenario design tasks with absolute numeric contracts are
given, under matched resources (same model, tools, task texts, and each
condition's standard budget), to three
decision-makers: a protocol-governed agent executing a five-stage multi-precision
funnel---whose closing stage reserves true first-principles computation for the
finalists---with mechanized adjudication and an evidence-chain audit ledger; a
protocol-free agent on the same harness; and a Bayesian optimizer configured to
the standard of the strongest comparable published agent framework. The governed
agent achieves all eight contracts under two DeepSeek-generation LLMs and
four of eight under a third, foreign-vendor model---whose four
non-attainments are honest not-achieved verdicts, never criterion
shifts. The Bayesian
optimizer achieves three---exactly those whose bottlenecks are structural. The
protocol-free agent achieves none within the same measurement space: of its
seven self-reported successes, two rest on a lithium-metal cell outside the
validated parameter sets and five dissolve under contract-caliber
re-adjudication---shifted thresholds, substituted models, self-set physical
parameters, altered calibers, missing criterion variables. Ablation
decomposes the governance
into task-dependent layers: the
ceiling-assessment-to-material-escalation mechanism is causal for two of three
material-bottleneck tasks, forced architecture exploration for one of six, and
three-model heterogeneous voting is an unrealized premium in this suite. The simulation world is anchored to a production cell: the
beginning-of-life $0.1$C discharge of a commercial LG M50T matches the calibrated
parameter set to $0.45\%$ capacity and $20.6$ mV RMSE---a validation of the simulator's
calibration against a production cell, distinct from any validation of the
designs. We conclude that the core
governance infrastructure for agent-mediated design is not the funnel but the
yardstick: pre-registered contracts, code-mechanical judgment, and audit chains
that make ``achieved'' a verifiable claim. The true-compute endorsement,
executed for the flagship molecular finalists, confirms the screening
verdicts by first principles in two independent codes (§5.8); and the
protocol, packaged as a
harness-independent declarative artifact, ran unchanged under three agent
frameworks with identical contract outcomes (§5.7). The protocol,
simulation library, adjudication layer, and experiment harnesses are open
source at \url{https://github.com/wangyuanxty/BatteryDesignAgent}.
\end{abstract}
