SHEET 06 · FINAL / 最终推荐

最终推荐

T10-final: OKane2022 (NMC811/graphite+SiOx) base + liquid cooling h=80 W/m2/K + advanced electrolyte (sigma=3.0 S/m, t+=0.6, D=4e-10 m2/s) + nanostructured anode (negative particle 0.8 um, positive 2.0 um). Achieved: ED 426.89 Wh/kg (>=392.61); 4C charge no plating (anode min +0.0341 V); T_max 324.55 K (<=333.15 K=60 C); overcharge to 4.70 V no thermal runaway (triggered=false). Design logic: graphite anode (Chen2020) plates fundamentally at 4C (anode -0.438 V, transport-insensitive); SiOx anode (OKane2022) does not plate but overheats at default cooling (T_max 368 K); liquid cooling caps T_max and advanced electrolyte transport + nanostructured anode restore the no-plating margin at the cooler operating temperature. All values DFN-verified (SPMe used only for screening).
