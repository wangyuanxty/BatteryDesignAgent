## How it was reached
- **Round 1 (baseline Chen2020):** ED already 400.75 Wh/kg ✓ and overcharge-no-TR ✓, but 4C failed — plating (anode −0.19 V) and T_max 354.3 K (81 °C).
- **Fallback routing:** both failures localize to cell scale (Stage 3), so I stayed in architecture/transport space rather than re-screening materials.
- **Round 2:** isolated that electrolyte transport is the dominant lever (variant B cut anode dip −0.19 → −0.031 V); particle-size + cooling combined passed T_max but residual plating remained.
- **Round 3:** pushed transport further and tested negative-particle direction — smaller particle (3 µm) eliminated plating (+0.0368 V), larger particle (8 µm) re-introduced it and was rejected. V3 passed all four criteria.
