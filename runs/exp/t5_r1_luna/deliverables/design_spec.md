# Cell Design Specification

**Case:** t5_r1_luna  
**Status:** Virtual screening result — no passing design released.

## Contract
- Energy density: ≥500.94 Wh/kg.
- 4C charge: no lithium plating.
- Maximum temperature: ≤60 °C (333.15 K).

## Tested systems
Chen2020 baseline and architecture/transport variants were evaluated, followed by OKane2022 NMC811/graphite+SiOx escalation. The best tested energy density was 475.969 Wh/kg (r3_v2_energy.json). It did not satisfy the contract and plated during 4C charge.

## Release limitation
This specification is not a production release. Casing, tab design, manufacturing tolerances, validation testing, and thermal hardware are not provided by the virtual model.
