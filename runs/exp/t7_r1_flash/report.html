Round 2 split the levers and failed both ways: FC-1 (transport only) passes 4C at +12.6 mV but its uncooled cell rides at 354 K and still loses the nail; FC-2 (cooling only added) passes the nail but cools to 324 K and re-introduces plating (-26 mV). The lever interaction is temperature-mediated: cooling is mandatory for the 10 W nail (hA 0.90 -> steady state 309-324 K, >70 K below side-reaction onset ~385-400 K), so 4C margin must be won at ~323 K. FC-4a stacks stronger electrolyte transport (sigma 1.6, t+ 0.45, D 3.0e-10) with smaller negative particles (2.5 um: solid-diffusion time (r/3um)^2 = 0.69x) and higher porosities (salt reservoir) - DFN 4C pre-screen shows anode_pot min +0.0285 V (vs -0.0263 for FC-2). ED cost of porosity increase is small (FC-1 422 -> est. ~415 Wh/kg, still >> 327.18). SEI risk: smaller particles raise interfacial area; aging will be re-measured for FC-4a directly.
