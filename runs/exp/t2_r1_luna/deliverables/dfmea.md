# Design FMEA (qualitative)

| Failure mode | Effect | Detection/control | Residual concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lithium plating during 4C charge | Capacity loss/safety risk | 4C coupled thermal/plating simulation; maintain anode potential ≥0 V | Validate with reference-electrode/operando testing |
| Excess SEI growth | Loss of lithium inventory and impedance rise | 100/500-cycle aging; target ≤500/≤550 nm | Model proxy needs experimental calibration |
| Low-temperature polarization | Reduced deliverable energy | −20 °C capacity retention test; target ≥90% | Cold-start and thermal conditioning not modeled |
| Transport parameter mismatch | Unexpected rate limitation | Measure conductivity, diffusivity, and t+ of proposed electrolyte | Overrides are not a bill of chemicals |
| Thermal rise at fast charge | Accelerated aging or safety event | Lumped thermal output recorded (348.786 K) | Cooling hardware/casing absent |
