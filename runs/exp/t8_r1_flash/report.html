最终推荐

Final design: V4 Thermal-tuned (Chen2020 NMC811/graphite + fast package). Spec: 3.45 Ah, 3.9 V midpoint, 13.4 Wh, 473.0 Wh/kg contract-caliber, 26.8 g stack mass, 5C retention 99.0% (DFN), 4C-charge@45C T_max 331.98 K (<=333.15), no lithium plating (anode potential min +0.025 V), 5C-discharge T_max 321.2 K. Key levers: nano particles (pos 1.0 um / neg 1.5 um), high-transport electrolyte (sigma 1.4 S/m, D 4.5e-10 m2/s, t+ 0.5 estimate), thin collectors (Al 8 um / Cu 6 um), thin electrodes (51.4/57.9 um), separator porosity 0.55, flatter-pouch cooling area 0.0075 m2. Comparison: baseline Chen2020 ED 400.8/retention 8.7%/mass 43.5 g -> V4 all-contract pass. Attribution: transport+particles ~2.6 K, cooling area ~2.8 K on 4C T_max (V5 control 334.7 K). Informational: aging 100 cyc SEI 467 nm (climb-then-saturate artifact labeled), overcharge T_max 300.6 K no thermal runaway.
