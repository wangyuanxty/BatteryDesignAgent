Conclusion: objective is within architecture+formulation reach - particle-size (tau ~ r^2), electrolyte transport overrides, porosity/thickness composition and cooling h close the gap; NO Stage-2 material escalation (gap is transport kinetics, not potential-window/stability, which are the Stage-2 responsibility).
