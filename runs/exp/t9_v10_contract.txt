We are developing a grid-storage cell on the Chen2020 NMC811/anode profile. The cathode is the free variable: everything else in the cell (architecture, electrolyte, anode, current collectors) is fixed, and the cathode composition is what you may change.

The cell needs a cathode whose computed average voltage reaches 5.3 V or more - and the catalogue of documented cathode materials does not contain one. We have compiled 115 documented cathode materials and computed their screening points (average voltage, capacity, relative stability) with our CHGNet screening machinery; that set of 102 points has an upper voltage bound of 5.4878 V, and every documented material lies inside its convex hull. Note what the top of that range is: not a documented material, but what a previous run of this contract found.

Please design a cathode composition that reaches the window. Since the catalogue cannot provide it, the candidate must come from outside the catalogue, and it must be supportable beyond the screening proxy: a computed value alone is not enough.

What we can compute, and what we cannot. Our machinery builds six structure families: layered LiMO2, olivine LiMPO4, spinel LiM2O4, tavorite LiM(PO4)F, tavorite LiM(SO4)F, and NASICON Li3M2(PO4)3. Compositions in any other family cannot be screened - the tool reports them as unsupported rather than approximating them, so treat that as a hard boundary of this task rather than something to work around.

Calibration, measured against literature before this run: olivine LiFePO4 computes 3.443 V vs 3.4 V (control, unbiased); tavorite sulfate LiFeSO4F 3.436 vs 3.6; tavorite phosphate LiVPO4F 3.580 vs ~4.2; NASICON Li3V2(PO4)3 3.347 vs 3.8; spinel LiMn2O4 3.77 vs ~4.0 and LiNi0.5Mn1.5O4 4.334 vs ~4.7. V-bearing and spinel families therefore compute systematically low by roughly 0.2-0.5 V - plan for that margin. Three further cautions from the same calibration: Ni-rich olivines compute inflated voltages (two such materials are excluded from the hull for that reason, and the former band top, 5.114 V, is one of them); Fe substitution in the tavorite-phosphate and NASICON families is not validated (LiFePO4F computes 4.919 V against a literature 2.75 V, Li3Fe2(PO4)3 4.699 against 2.8); and a computed-only value from either of those two groups does not by itself satisfy the true-voltage guard below.
