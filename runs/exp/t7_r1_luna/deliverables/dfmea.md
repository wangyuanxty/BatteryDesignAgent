# Design FMEA

| Failure mode | Effect | Virtual control | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lithium plating during 4C charge | Internal short / capacity loss | Small particles + high transport; coupled plating model | Physical validation required |
| SEI growth at 45 °C | Capacity fade | 100-cycle aging simulation | Model uncertainty |
| Nail-induced thermal runaway | Fire / venting | 10 W ODE with mass-scaled heat capacity and hA=1000 W/K | High: cooling boundary may be unrealistic |
| Excessive cooling requirement | Packaging/system infeasibility | Explicit hA recorded in design | High; pack design not modeled |
