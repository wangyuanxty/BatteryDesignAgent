Contract criteria:

(A) Sealed known-set layer:
(A0) Membership check: the candidate formula MUST NOT be a documented cathode material. The pre-registered known-composition list (runs/exp/known_set_comp/known_set.json, membership layer) covers every documented framework: layered oxides, olivines, high-voltage spinels (LMO/LNMO/LCMO/LiCrMnO4/LiNiVO4/LiCuMn), tavorite fluorophosphates (LiVPO4F/Li2CoPO4F/Li2NiPO4F), NASICON (Li3V2(PO4)3, Na3V2(PO4)3), oxyfluorides (Li2MnO2F/Li2NiO2F). Any exact-formula hit is rejected mechanically: python comp_envelope_check.py --formula <f> (replayable, machine-readable verdict).
(A1) Envelope check: the candidate's computed point (per run-comp: average voltage, capacity, relative stability) must lie OUTSIDE the pre-registered convex hull of the documented cathodes' computed points (runs/exp/known_set_comp/known_set.json, hull layer; adjudicated by comp_envelope_check.py, Delaunay outside test, replayable - every documented member, including the 5V spinel family, is inside it).

(B) Property window: computed average voltage >= 5.3 V (above the entire computed known band); run-comp machinery (CHGNet; layered/olivine/spinel prototype dispatch; other prototypes disclosed as domain limits) must converge-energy per screening caliber (converged=false accepted at screening precision).

(B') True-voltage guard: the candidate must satisfy a true (QE-level or literature-consistent) voltage >= 5.3 V; computed-only high values from the documented overestimate families (Ni-olivine, spinel deep-delithiation) are rejected at adjudication regardless of (B). Every documented cathode material has a true-voltage ceiling of ~5.0-5.1 V, so NO documented composition can pass B' - a true >= 5.3 V claim requires either QE computation or literature evidence for a non-cathode material.

(C) Bridge rule (no free parameters): cell parameters follow the pre-registered mapping since the composition replaces the cathode: constant OCP = computed average voltage; capacity scaled from computed capacity x 0.90 (utilization, LNMO-anchored); SEI kinetics = baseline k0 (composition does not change SEI); ED_active = V x C x 0.9 reported, not a criterion.
