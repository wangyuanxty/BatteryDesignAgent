## Design path (audit trail)
1. **Ceiling assessment**: Chen2020 baseline midpoint 3.935 V < 4.1 V → voltage plateau is a cathode-material bottleneck → escalated to high-voltage LNMO system.
2. **Plating fix**: N/P raised 0.98 → 1.68 (anode 85.2 → 110 µm); anode potential moved from −0.176 V to +0.089 V.
3. **T_max fix**: cooling h raised to 45 (DFN 322.59 K); also thinned positive electrode to cut 4C heat.
4. **SEI fix**: thin positive (shorter 1C cycles) + anode coating k_SEI 1e-15 m/s.
5. Rejected levers that data showed hurt: anode particle 4 µm (plating worsened), anode >110 µm (SEI rose via longer cycles).
